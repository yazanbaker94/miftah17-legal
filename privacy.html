Privacy Policy

Miftah17 Poster, last updated 2 November 2026

Miftah17 Poster is used only by Miftah 17 Studios to publish videos that the studio itself made to the studio's own Facebook Page and Instagram professional account.

What data we use

The tool uses an access token for the studio's own accounts so it can upload videos, captions and covers, and read the status of those uploads. It does not collect, store or process personal data of any other person.

What we do not do

We do not read messages, comments or follower data, we do not sell or share any data, and we do not use any data for advertising.

Storage

The access token is stored only on the studio's own computer. Upload logs (video file name, time, and the post ID Meta returns) are kept on the same computer.

Deletion

See the Data Deletion page.

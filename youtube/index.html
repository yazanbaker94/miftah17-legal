Miftah17 - Updated 2 November 2026

Miftah17 is a private publishing tool operated by Miftah 17 Studios for its own YouTube channel. It is used by the studio's authorized operator and is not a public sign-up service.

What the tool does

With the channel owner's Google authorization, Miftah17 uploads and schedules videos, sets titles and descriptions, uploads thumbnails, checks processing and publication status, and reads or manages channel comments when requested by the owner.

The operator supplies the videos, artwork, metadata and publication times. The tool does not obtain ownership of the channel or its content.

Google authorization

Sign-in and consent take place on Google's own pages. Access can be revoked at any time through Google Account permissions.
